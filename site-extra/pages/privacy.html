Privacy policy

How Docxels handles your information

Last updated: 23 October 2026 (accounts, writing assistant, Pro, anonymous usage counts, update notices)

1. Who we are

Docxels is a product of Pixels Limited ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Docxels app and this website. Questions and requests go to docxels@pixelslimited.ng.

2. Your documents stay on your phone

Documents you scan, create, import or edit are stored on your phone, in the app's private storage, encrypted with a key kept in the Android Keystore. Scanning, straightening, clean-up, automatic rotation, text recognition, the QR and barcode reader, editing, converting PDF to Word (and Word to PDF), the PDF tools and exporting all work on the phone. The text recognition and barcode models run on the phone, and the file you choose to convert is read only by the app. We do not receive your documents. The only text from a document that ever leaves the phone is text you choose to send to the writing assistant (section 5).

3. When Docxels uses the internet

Docxels uses the internet only for the things below. Scanning, editing and exporting never need it.

  • Updates and news. About once a day the app checks for messages from us, and it looks for a new version when you open it, about every 10 minutes while it is open, and in the background about every 15 minutes. It reads a small version file from the Docxels release page hosted on GitHub. A new version is downloaded and installed only when you tap Update; where possible only the changes are downloaded, and the app checks the result against its fingerprint and signature before installing. Like any web request, the host can see your device's IP address. You can switch this off with "Announcements and update notices" in Settings (see section 6).
  • Anonymous usage counts (section 6), sent with the daily check.
  • Your account, only if you create one (section 4).
  • The writing assistant, only when you tap one of its actions while signed in (section 5).
  • Docxels Pro, only if you choose to upgrade: the payment page opens in your browser (section 4).
  • The cut-out helper. The small model used by Remove background is downloaded from the Docxels release page the first time you use it.

4. Your account and payments

An account is optional. Everything except the writing assistant and Pro works without one.

If you create an account we keep your email address, your first and last name and your password. The password is stored in protected form by our account service, Supabase, so we cannot read it. We send the confirmation and password-reset emails. We also keep which plan you are on (free or Pro) and, for Pro, the date it runs to. Your name is shown in the app and is written as the owner of documents you create.

Payments for Pro are taken on Paystack's own payment page. Card and bank details are entered with Paystack and never reach us. We keep only the payment reference, the amount, the currency, the status and the dates, so that we can switch Pro on and answer questions about a payment.

5. The writing assistant

When you are signed in and tap an assistant action (rewrite, shorten, expand, translate, fix, summarise, ask a question, clean scanned text, suggest a name, check writing, or, with Pro, write a letter or document), the text you chose, or the whole document if nothing is selected, is sent to our server and from there to Anthropic's Claude, which writes the reply. We do not keep that text or the reply. We keep only a count of how many requests each account made each day and how many tokens they used, to apply the daily limit. Do not send text you have no right to share. The assistant is never used on a document unless you tap one of its actions.

6. Anonymous usage counts

With the daily check the app sends: a random install code (not linked to your name, email or account), the app build number, the Android version, the phone brand and model, the language, the names of features used with how many times (for example "scan" or "pdf_tool:merge"), and short codes for errors and crashes. Your country is worked out from the request. Your IP address is turned into a one-way scrambled value used only to stop abuse, and that value is deleted after about two days. Document names, text, files, your name and your email are never included. Switching off "Announcements and update notices" in Settings stops the counting and the daily check.

7. Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: to scan documents and read QR codes. Photos are used only when you take or pick them.
  • Files and photos: to import documents and pictures you choose, and to save or share exports where you decide. "All files access" is optional: you can turn it on in Settings so that Documents search can list PDF, Word and text files anywhere on the phone.
  • Internet and network state: for the uses in section 3, and to run the update check only when you are online.
  • Notifications: to tell you a new version is ready.
  • Install apps: to hand a downloaded, verified update to Android's installer.
  • Fingerprint, face or screen lock: only if you turn on app lock. Android's own prompt does the check; Docxels never receives your biometric data or PIN, only whether the check passed.

8. Saved letterheads, signatures and settings

Letterheads and signatures you add in Settings (from a picture, a PDF or a Word file; the app finds the artwork or signature inside the file on your phone and leaves the letter text out) are kept on your phone in the app's private storage, encrypted with the same Keystore key as your documents. They are never uploaded. Your look-and-feel choices, app-lock options and the fact that you accepted our terms are saved in the app's private settings on the phone.

9. Sharing and exports

When you export or share a document it goes only where you send it, using Android's share sheet or a folder you choose. Exported scan pages carry a small "Scanned with Docxels by Pixels" line at the bottom right (Pro members can switch it off). Once a file is outside the app, its protection depends on where you put it.

10. Screenshots

Screenshots are allowed by default. In Settings you can turn on "Hide screen contents", which blocks screenshots and screen recording inside Docxels and hides the app in the recent-apps list. Docxels does not capture or send your screen.

11. Other companies that handle information

GitHub hosts the update files and the cut-out model. Supabase runs accounts and our servers. Anthropic writes the assistant's replies. Paystack takes payments. Each handles only what is described above, under its own terms. We do not sell your information and the app contains no advertising.

12. Security

We use the platform's secure storage for the encryption key, keep document contents out of logs, check files before opening them, switch off Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer for the app, and show a local-only warning if the phone looks rooted or debugged (nothing is reported anywhere). Our account tables are locked so that each person can see only their own records, and our admin page can be opened only by the Pixels Limited owner. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your phone protected with a screen lock and choose a strong password.

13. Keeping and deleting data

Deleting a document in the app moves it to Trash and removes it after 30 days or when you empty the Trash. "Delete all data" in Settings erases every document, letterhead, signature and setting on the phone. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. Files you exported to Downloads or Pictures are yours to delete. To have your account and its plan, payment and usage records deleted, email us from the address on the account.

14. Your rights

You may ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it or delete it, and ask us to stop using it, by emailing docxels@pixelslimited.ng. Because anonymous usage counts are not linked to you, we cannot pick out yours; switching them off in Settings stops them.

15. Children

Docxels is a general-purpose tool and is not aimed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has made an account, email us and we will delete it.

16. Fonts and open-source software

The app's interface uses the Inter typeface (SIL Open Font License 1.1, © The Inter Project Authors), bundled inside the app, so no font is downloaded. Other open-source components include PdfBox-Android (Apache 2.0) for PDF tools, OpenCV (Apache 2.0) for image processing, and Google ML Kit for on-device text and barcode recognition. Full notices are in the app under Settings, About. Fonts named in Word documents (for example Calibri) are stored as names only; Docxels does not include Microsoft fonts.

17. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app and ask you to accept it again.

18. Contact us

Questions or requests: docxels@pixelslimited.ng.
