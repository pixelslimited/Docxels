What are we working on?

Scans, Word files and PDFs live side by side, so a scan can become a Word document, and a Word document a signed PDF, in a couple of taps.

Scan

Point, shoot, done. Docxels finds the paper, straightens it and makes it look like a clean photocopy.

  • Finds the page edges by itself and straightens the page
  • Automatic clean-up: shadows, colour cast and grey paper edges go; handwriting stays
  • Colour, grey or black-and-white photocopy looks
  • Pages turn upright on their own
  • Reduce noise, sharpen and reduce glare when a shot needs help
  • Scan a whole stack, then reorder, rotate, duplicate or mirror pages
  • Reads the text with on-device OCR
  • Flash, grid, self-timer and volume-key shutter

Write

A Word-style editor in your pocket. Open, edit and save .docx, with the fonts the document was written in.

  • Pages look like real paper in nine sizes, with pinch zoom and a dark-paper night mode
  • Tables you type straight into, drag into place and resize with a corner handle
  • Pictures, shapes and text boxes with Word-style text wrap, resize and rotate
  • Headers, footers, page numbers, footnotes, hyperlinks and a styles gallery
  • Comments, track changes and version history
  • Mail merge, form fields (check boxes, dates, drop-downs) and saved snippets
  • Spell check, dictate and read aloud
  • Search every command with "What do you want to do?"
  • The editor, the preview and the exported PDF use one layout, so what you see is what you get

Questions

Short, honest answers.

Does Docxels upload my documents?

No. Your documents stay on your phone, encrypted. The only text that leaves it is text you choose to send to the writing assistant, and that needs an account.

Can I turn a scan or a PDF into a Word file?

Yes. PDFs with text keep their bold, italics, tables, pictures and letterhead artwork; scans are read with on-device OCR and open as an editable document. OCR can make mistakes, so check important text.

Will my Word files look the same?

Docxels reads and writes standard .docx files, with the document's own fonts. Simple and typical business documents carry across well; some complex layouts, such as text flowing between columns, are not fully supported yet.

Do I need an account?

No. An account is only for the writing assistant and Pro. You can delete it yourself in the app whenever you like.

What if I lose my phone?

Pages are encrypted on the phone and you can lock the app with your fingerprint, face or screen lock. These protections help against someone reaching the stored files; they cannot protect a phone that is already unlocked.
