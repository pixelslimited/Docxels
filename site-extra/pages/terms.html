The rules for using Docxels

Last updated: 23 October 2026 (accounts, writing assistant, Pro, export credit, updates)

1. Accepting these terms

When you first open Docxels you are asked to accept these terms and our privacy policy; the app cannot be used until you do. If you do not agree, please exit and uninstall the app. If we change these terms in a way you must agree to, you will be asked again.

2. The app

Pixels Limited grants you a personal, non-exclusive licence to use Docxels on your devices. You may not copy, resell or reverse engineer the app except where the law allows it.

3. Your content

You own your documents. They are kept on your phone and we do not receive them; the only text that leaves the phone is text you choose to send to the writing assistant. We claim no rights over your documents. You are responsible for the content you scan, create and share, and for having the right to use it.

4. Automatic results

Page detection, straightening, clean-up, automatic rotation, text recognition, QR and barcode reading, PDF to Word and Scan to Word conversion, table detection and the writing assistant are automatic and can make mistakes. Always check the result before relying on it, especially for contracts, financial or official documents. Your original scan or PDF is never replaced by the converted result. Pictures, text styles and repeated letterhead artwork are carried over where the app can find them, but drawings and lines, some formatting and unsupported parts of a Word file (comments, tracked changes, text wrapped around objects) may not be, and the app tells you when it knows this has happened. Handwriting on a scan is kept.

5. Signatures and letterheads

A signature you draw in Docxels is an image placed on a document. It is not a cryptographic digital signature, and whether it is legally binding depends on the document and the law where you are. You can also add a signature or letterhead from a picture, a PDF or a Word file and keep it in Settings. Only use signatures and letterheads that you are entitled to use.

6. Your account

An account is optional and is needed only for the writing assistant and Pro. You must give a real email address, confirm it, and keep your password to yourself; you are responsible for what is done with your account. You can sign out at any time and can ask us to delete your account (see the privacy policy). We may suspend an account that breaks these terms.

7. The writing assistant

The assistant is available when you are signed in. It works on the text you choose, or the whole document if nothing is selected, and sends it to our server and to Anthropic's Claude to write the reply (see the privacy policy). Its answers can be wrong, out of date or unsuitable: check them, and do not rely on them as legal, medical, financial or professional advice. There is a daily limit on requests, and some actions, such as writing a letter or document, are part of Pro. Do not send text you have no right to share.

8. Docxels Pro and payments

Docxels is free to start. Pro is paid. The price, billing period and what Pro includes are shown in the app before you pay, and we may change them for future purchases. Payment is taken on Paystack's page; Pro is switched on once Paystack confirms the payment and lasts until the end date shown in your profile. If something goes wrong with a payment, email us with the payment reference.

9. The Docxels credit on exported pages

Exported scan pages carry a small "Scanned with Docxels by Pixels" credit with the Docxels and Pixels logos at the bottom right. Pro members can switch it off in Settings. Please do not remove it in any other way from pages exported with it on.

10. Updates and notices

Docxels checks for news and new versions, as described in the privacy policy, and installs an update only when you tap Update. You can switch these checks off in Settings, and then update by hand. We may stop supporting old versions.

11. Acceptable use

Do not use Docxels to break the law, infringe others' rights, process documents you have no right to handle, forge or falsely sign documents, send harmful or unlawful content to the assistant, try to get around the daily limits or the Pro checks, or interfere with our servers or other people's use of the app.

12. Changes to the app

We may add, change or remove features, and some features may need an account or Pro. We will tell you in the app about significant changes.

13. Disclaimers and liability

Docxels is provided "as is". We work to keep your files safe, but your documents live on your phone, so please keep backups of important documents. To the extent the law allows, Pixels Limited is not liable for indirect losses or loss of data, or for decisions you make from automatic results or the assistant's answers.

14. Ending

You can stop using Docxels at any time by uninstalling it. We may suspend or end your access if you break these terms. Sections that by their nature continue (such as 3, 4 and 13) continue after that.

15. Changes to these terms

If we change these terms we will update the date above and, for significant changes, ask you to accept them again in the app.
