Scan paper. Get clean documents. Keep them private.

Docxels turns photos of paper into straight, clean PDFs, Word and Excel files, right on your Android phone. It works offline and your documents never leave the device.

What it does today

Straight, flat pages

Finds the page edges, corrects perspective and tilt, and flattens curved pages.

Clean results

Colour that stays true, grey, or a black-and-white look like a clean photocopy.

Remove clutter

Take out fingers, stray objects, staples and folded corners.

Text and tables

On-device OCR to Word or text. Detected tables export to Excel, Word, PDF or image.

Private by design

No internet permission, encrypted storage, optional fingerprint lock, hidden screen contents.

Share anywhere

Export PDF, images, Word, Excel or text and send them with your usual apps.

On the way

Word processor in development

Write and edit documents with formatting, tables and pictures, and open or save Microsoft Word (.docx) files.

PDF tools planned

Merge, split, reorder, annotate, fill forms and add your signature.

Questions

Does Docxels upload my documents?

No. Scanning, cleaning and text recognition all run on your phone, and the app does not ask for internet access.

Can I get Word and Excel files from a scan?

Yes. Text is read with on-device OCR and saved as a Word file. Tables can be exported to Excel. OCR can make mistakes, so check important text.

What if I lose my phone?

Pages are encrypted on the phone and you can lock the app with your fingerprint, face or screen lock. These protections help against someone getting at the stored files; they cannot protect a phone that is already unlocked.
