Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026. Docxels is a product of Pixels Limited ("we").

The short version

Docxels works on your phone. We do not collect your documents, we have no user accounts, and the app contains no advertising or analytics. The app does not request internet access.

What the app handles

  • Photos and scans. Pages you capture or import are stored in the app's private storage on your phone, encrypted with a key kept in the Android Keystore.
  • Text recognition. Text is read on your phone with a model bundled in the app. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • Camera. Used only to take the photos you ask for. Photos from your gallery are read only when you pick them.
  • Fingerprint, face or screen lock. If you turn on app lock, Android's own prompt checks it's you. Docxels never receives your fingerprint, face data or PIN; it only learns whether the check passed.
  • Settings. Your choices (such as app lock) are saved on the phone.

Sharing and exports

When you export or share a document, it goes only where you send it, using Android's share sheet or by saving it to your Downloads or Pictures folder. Once a file is outside the app, its protection depends on where you put it.

Backups

Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are switched off for this app, so your documents are not copied to Google's or anyone else's servers by the system.

Deleting your data

Deleting a document in the app removes its pages from the phone. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. Files you exported to Downloads or Pictures are yours to delete.

Children

Docxels is a general-purpose tool and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If we add online features such as backup or AI help, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent before any document leaves your phone.
