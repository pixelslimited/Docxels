## Full description (4000 max)

Docxels turns your camera into a private document scanner.

Take a photo of any page and Docxels finds the edges, straightens it and cleans it up. Choose true colour, grey, or a black-and-white look like a clean photocopy.

SCAN BETTER
• Automatic page detection and perspective correction
• Curved or bent pages flattened
• Fingers, stray objects, staples and folded corners removed
• Auto-capture, multi-page documents, book spreads split into two pages
• Rearrange, rotate, crop and retake pages

TURN SCANS INTO FILES
• Export to PDF or images
• Text recognition (OCR) into a Word file or plain text
• Tables detected and exported to Excel, Word, PDF or image
• Share with any app

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works fully offline. The app does not ask for internet access
• Pages are encrypted on your phone
• Optional app lock with fingerprint, face or screen lock
• Screenshots and recent-apps previews hidden
• No ads, no accounts, no tracking

COMING SOON (soon)
• Write and edit documents with a Word-compatible editor
• PDF tools: merge, split, annotate, fill forms and sign

Text recognition and table detection are automatic and can make mistakes — check important documents.

Docxels is a product of Pixels Limited.
